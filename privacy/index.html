Privacy

How Motsac handles your data.

Key statements

Reads only messages you explicitly select
Motsac analyzes only the messages you highlight or select. Nothing else.
No background scraping
We don't read your chats in the background. No automatic scanning.
No auto-sending
Nothing is sent automatically. You review every suggestion and decide what to send.
No full chat storage
We don't store your entire chat history. Only the messages you select for analysis are processed.
No access without user action
Motsac only works when you actively select a message. No passive monitoring.

You stay in control

Every step requires your action. You choose what to analyze, you review suggestions, and you decide what to send.

Questions?

If you have privacy concerns or questions, please contact us.
