How it works

Transparent process. You stay in control.

Step 1: Select a message

Highlight a message in your Telegram chat that you want to analyze or improve.

Step 2: Analyze context

Tone
How your words feel to the recipient.
Power balance
Who has more influence in this conversation.
Emotional signals
Signals inferred from wording and context.

Step 3: See suggestions

Best approaches (+%)
Options that improve the contact and communication.
What not to say (–%)
Phrases that might worsen the relationship or stall the chat.

Step 4: You decide what to send

Motsac shows you options. The final text and decision are always yours.
