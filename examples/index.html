Examples

See how Motsac improves your messages.

Cold contact

Before
"Hi, I saw your post. Can we talk?"
After
"Hi, your post about [specific topic] caught my attention. I'm working on something similar. Would you be open to a quick chat?"
Why it works
Shows you read their content, explains your reason, and asks permission instead of demanding attention.

Negotiation

Before
"Your price is too high. Can you lower it?"
After
"I understand your pricing. My budget is [amount]. Is there flexibility, or should we explore alternatives?"
Why it works
Acknowledges their position, states yours clearly, and opens options instead of just demanding a discount.

Personal conversation

Before
"Sorry I didn't reply earlier. I was busy."
After
"Sorry for the delay. I wanted to give your message proper attention. [Your actual response]"
Why it works
Shows respect for their message instead of just making excuses. Then moves to the actual reply.

Important

These are examples, not guarantees. Every conversation is unique. Motsac helps you see options, but context matters most.
